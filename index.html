How this maps to your APK

1
Settings screen

Mirrors activity_main.xml — url_input and btn_save exactly as declared in your layout.

2
Apply as Wallpaper

On device this launches the system's ACTION_CHANGE_LIVE_WALLPAPER intent, pointing at your WebWallpaperService from the manifest.

3
Wallpaper render

The right phone simulates the hardware-accelerated WebView your service draws behind the home screen icons and clock.

!
Browser limits

This is an approximation — real device WebViews allow broader permissions (camera, sensors, local files) that a sandboxed iframe here cannot.
