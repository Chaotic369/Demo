How this maps to your APK

1
Settings screen

Mirrors activity_main.xml — UI inputs exactly as declared in your layout.

2
Apply as Wallpaper

On device this launches the system's ACTION_CHANGE_LIVE_WALLPAPER intent, pointing at your service.

3
Wallpaper render

The right phone simulates the hardware-accelerated view your service draws behind the home screen.

!
Browser limits

This is an approximation — real device views allow broader permissions that a sandboxed frame here cannot.

4
Keyboard Hack

If toggled, the service intercepts touches to launch a transparent Activity, stealing window focus to force the system keyboard to appear over the wallpaper.

5
Loop Mode

Controls how video wallpapers restart — straight loop back to the first frame, or bounce/ping-pong playback in reverse before looping again.

6
Refresh on Reconnect

If the device loses and regains network connectivity, this reloads the wallpaper source automatically instead of leaving a stale or broken frame.

7
Max Resolution / Framerate Cap

Limits how much GPU work the wallpaper does by capping render resolution and frame updates, trading visual smoothness for battery life.

8
Screen Timeout Behavior

Decides whether the wallpaper keeps rendering while the screen is locked, or pauses entirely to save power until the device wakes again.
